    Questions

    How long does delivery take?

    Usually 30–45 minutes. Every batata is roasted to order over charcoal.

    Do you have options without nuts?

    Yes. The Classic and Om Ali can be made nut-free. Just add it in the notes.

    Can I order for a party?
