    Questions

    How long does delivery take?

    Usually 30–45 minutes. Every batata is roasted to order over charcoal.

    How do I pay?

    Pay cash to the driver, or pay online by card when that option is shown.

    Do you have options without nuts?

    Yes. The Classic and Om Ali can be made nut-free. Just add it in the notes.

    Can I order for a party?

    Yes. For 10 or more pieces, place your order a day ahead and mention it in the notes.
